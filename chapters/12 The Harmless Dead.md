This was a new development.  I reached in my jacket pocket, pulled out my harmonica, and tried to put it in Phil's hand. “Here, buddy, you play?”

He, apparently did not.  After three attempts, and three times being ignored, I grabbed it and started improving “Take the F Train" while motioning for Eugene to

Abruptly Phil tossed Eugene into the wall, stunning him just long enough that the zombie sank his teeth into the kid's shoulder.  He shrieked in pain. Phil growled like a wild animal and reared his head  at me, Eugene's forearm still dangling from his dislocated jaw.  Slowly he stood up and began walking towards me, arms hanging limply at his side.  I ran into the hotel, rounded the corner, and pressed the button to summon the freight elevator at least a dozen times before running back to the loading dock.
