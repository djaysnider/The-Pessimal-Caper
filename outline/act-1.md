Thursday evening

Jack arrives at the hotel, meets Reggie ... introduce "Slugger" and Luk See ... start setting up something mysterious. Reggie shows him where the jewels are kept. Jack hypothesizes ways to steal them, good and bad. Slugger and Luk overhear him.

Jack's rant ... Yakuza vs american gangsters

## Chapter 5 — Weatherby

Friday Morning

He promises to give them the room back in plenty of time to set out silverware or whatever it is they do while he makes art.”

## Chapter 6 — Debutantes escalate

Friday afternoon

Sally talks to the other debutantes. Gets the idea there's a conspiracy involving Sally's family.  Tells jack.

Jack is tired of humoring Sally. Finally decides to prove to her the jewels are still there.  Discoveres they have been stolen and replaced with a fake, just as he suggested the day before when he was surrounded by underworld criminals.
